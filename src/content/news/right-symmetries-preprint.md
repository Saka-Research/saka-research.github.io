---
title: "New preprint: What are the Right Symmetries for Formal Theorem Proving?"
date: 2026-05-21
summary: "A category-theoretic framework for the symmetries that LLM-based theorem provers should respect."
---

LLM-based theorem provers can succeed or fail on statements that are mathematically equivalent. In [What are the Right Symmetries for Formal Theorem Proving?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22257), led by Krzysztof Olejniczak, we introduce rewriting categories to formalise which symmetries provers should respect, such as proof equivariance and success invariance.
