---
title: "New preprint: MUX, continuous reasoning via multiplexed tokens"
date: 2026-05-19
summary: "MUX compresses step-by-step reasoning of language models into continuous latent tokens that each multiplex a span of reasoning."
---

In [MUX: Continuous Reasoning via Multiplexed Tokens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18264), led by Ayhan Suleymanzade, language models reason with continuous latent tokens, each a lossless superposition of a span of discrete reasoning steps, making reasoning more compact and higher-bandwidth.
