In August 2025, İsmail İlkan Ceylan joined the [Technical University of Vienna](https://www.tuwien.at/) as Assistant Professor and [AITHYRA](https://www.aithyra.at/), the research institute for biomedical artificial intelligence, as Adjunct PI. He remains an Academic Member at the University of Oxford, where he was Assistant Professor from 2020 to 2025.
