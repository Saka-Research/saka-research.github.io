Our paper [MUX: Continuous Reasoning via Multiplexed Tokens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18264) by Ayhan Suleymanzade, Halil Alperen Gozeten, Michael M. Bronstein, İsmail İlkan Ceylan, and Jinwoo Kim was accepted to NeurIPS 2026 and selected as a spotlight.
