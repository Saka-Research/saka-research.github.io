---
title: "New preprint: Global Transport Couplings for Classifier-Free Guided Flows"
date: 2026-10-06
summary: "A class-agnostic optimal-transport coupling for conditional flow models that consistently improves generation when combined with classifier-free guidance."
---

In [Global Transport Couplings for Classifier-Free Guided Flows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07555), led by Katarina Petrović, we introduce Global Transport, an optimal-transport coupling computed without class labels. On its own it hurts conditional generation, but combined with classifier-free guidance it consistently improves results across domains, model scales, and sampling budgets.
