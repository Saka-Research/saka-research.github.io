Three papers with group members were accepted to ICLR 2026:

- [Topological Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15897), led by Kacper Wyrwal
- [HYPER: A Foundation Model for Inductive Link Prediction with Knowledge Hypergraphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12362), led by Xingyue Huang
- [Flock: A Knowledge Graph Foundation Model via Learning on Random Walks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01510), with Xingyue Huang and Krzysztof Olejniczak
