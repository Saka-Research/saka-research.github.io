Chemical bonds and molecular shapes, atomic arrangements in crystals, and interactions between cells provide rich relational and geometric structure for machine learning.
Together with our colleagues at [AITHYRA](https://www.aithyra.at/), we develop methods that use this structure to model and generate scientific systems, with a particular focus on the life sciences.
